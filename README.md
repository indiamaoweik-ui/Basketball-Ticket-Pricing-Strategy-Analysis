# Basketball Ticket Pricing Strategy Analysis

## Project Overview

Analyzed 30,001 basketball ticket records using Microsoft Excel to evaluate ticket pricing, demand, and revenue performance across different seating sections.

The goal was to assess pricing strategies and determine whether current pricing, dynamic pricing, and discounting practices were affecting revenue performance.

## Business Questions

The analysis was designed to answer three key business questions:

1. **Are Upper Bowl seats overpriced?**
2. **Does dynamic pricing increase revenue?**
3. **Is discounting hurting premium sections?**

## Data & Tools

- **Data:** Basketball Ticket Pricing Dataset
- **Records:** 30,001
- **Tool:** Microsoft Excel
- **Analysis:** Pivot Tables, formulas, visualizations, and KPIs
- **Pivot Tables:** 12
- **Visualizations:** 12
- **KPIs:** 7

## Analytical Process

I first organized the ticket data and used Excel `IF` formulas to classify tickets into four seating categories: VIP, Courtside, Lower Bowl, and Upper Bowl.

I then used Pivot Tables, visualizations, and KPIs to compare ticket pricing, sell-through, revenue per seat, and performance across seating sections and pricing conditions.

The analysis examined Upper Bowl pricing, the relationship between dynamic pricing and revenue, and the effect of discounts on premium seating sections.

Rather than relying on a single metric, I compared demand and revenue together to better understand whether pricing changes were actually improving financial performance.

## Key Insights

- Upper Bowl tickets had a **76% sell-through rate**, compared with an **88% average** and **95% benchmark**.
- Despite the lower sell-through rate, Upper Bowl tickets generated **$199.33 in revenue per seat**, compared with an **$80 average**.
- Adjusted sell-through was **75.4%**, compared with **73.8% for non-adjusted tickets**.
- The Upper Bowl results highlighted the importance of evaluating both revenue and demand when determining whether seats are overpriced.
- The analysis also evaluated whether dynamic pricing increased revenue and whether discounting negatively affected premium seating performance.
